HackCal Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

What we store

  • Your Google account email address, and Google’s account ID for you, so we know who you are when you sign in.
  • A Google refresh token, so HackCal can update your HackCal calendar every hour. It is stored encrypted (AES-256-GCM).
  • Your filter settings, such as mode, home city and radius, minimum prize, topics and contest platforms.
  • The IDs of the calendar events HackCal created in your HackCal calendar, so we can update or remove them and never re-add one you deleted.
  • Any exams or deadlines you add yourself through HackCal.

We don’t store your name, profile photo, contacts or anything from your other calendars.

What we access in your Google account

HackCal asks only for your email address and the calendar.app.created permission. With it, HackCal creates one secondary calendar called “HackCal” and only ever creates, changes or deletes events inside that calendar. It can’t see or change your other calendars or their events.

How we use it

We use this data only to run HackCal for you: to keep your HackCal calendar in sync with the hackathons and contests that match your settings. We never sell your data, never use it for advertising, and never share it with anyone except the services that host HackCal (the web host and the database provider), which store it on our behalf.

HackCal’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

How to delete everything

  • Click Disconnect in HackCal’s settings. This deletes your account and everything listed above from our database and revokes HackCal’s access to your Google account. The HackCal calendar stays in your Google Calendar until you delete it yourself.
  • Or email mohitgalba@gmail.com and we will delete your data.

You can also remove HackCal’s access at any time from your Google account’s third-party connections page.
