HackCal

HackCal is a free tool for students that puts hackathons (from Brabble and Devpost) and coding contests (Codeforces, LeetCode, CodeChef and AtCoder) into a separate “HackCal” Google Calendar, filtered by the preferences you choose: mode, city and distance, prize, topics and contest platforms. You can also add your own exams and deadlines to it. HackCal creates that one calendar in your Google account and only ever reads or changes events inside it; your other calendars are never touched.
